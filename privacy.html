02

Information the app handles

Game progress on your device

The app stores your player name, virtual wallet, game results and history, free spins, unfinished supported hands, favourites, rewards, achievements and preferences in its private local storage. Purchase-delivery records help prevent duplicate coin credits. These game saves are not offered as a cloud backup or cross-device service.

Installation analytics

On the first opening of an installation, the app makes one background attempt to send installation information to our analytics service at agile-data-api.top. This includes the Google Advertising ID when available, the app-scoped Android ID, the full Google Play install referrer, Android version, locale, device model and build identifier. A zero value replaces an unavailable or restricted Advertising ID. The server also receives network information such as the IP address used to connect.

The request is not repeated on later openings of the same installation, including after a failed attempt. Clearing app data or reinstalling creates a new installation. This one-time rule applies to installation analytics; the other services below may communicate throughout app use.

App usage and advertising events

Google Analytics for Firebase processes app interactions and standard app/device information. The app records House Ads impressions, clicks, closes and reward claims, including placement, creative identifier, media format, promoted app, watch duration and close reason.

Crash and performance diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, supported app-not-responding reports, installation identifiers and device/app diagnostics. Firebase Performance Monitoring measures startup, supported rendering and network performance, including connection timings and technical request metadata. The app does not intentionally add purchase tokens, Advertising IDs, Android IDs or request bodies to custom diagnostic reports.

Notifications

OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging process push subscription and delivery information, including push tokens, service-generated identifiers, permission status and device/app details. Network connections may expose an IP address. OneSignal initializes when the app starts; declining notification permission prevents permitted notification display but does not disable other analytics or necessarily prevent subscription-related processing.

Purchases and support

Google Play handles payment details. The app receives purchase information such as product IDs, order identifiers, purchase tokens, quantities and purchase status to deliver virtual coins and avoid duplicate delivery. The app does not receive your full payment-card details. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include.

Retention and deletion

Local game data

Local progress and purchase-delivery records remain on the device until removed through Android’s app-storage controls or by uninstalling. Doing this removes your wallet and progress and does not restore already consumed coin packs. You can change your player name and preferences inside the app.

Information already sent to services

Uninstalling or clearing local data does not automatically delete information already transmitted to our server, Google or OneSignal. Service-side data is retained according to the purposes described here, provider retention settings and applicable requirements. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and follow-up; records may be retained longer where needed for legal obligations, security or disputes. Provider-specific retention information is available in the notices linked above.

To request access, correction or deletion of information we can associate with you, email uxokapidagal12@gmail.com. Describe the request and how you used the app. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details. We may request proportionate information to locate relevant records and verify the request. Some information may need to be retained where applicable requirements permit or require it.
